PrayerStop Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · PrayerStop is developed by Ahamed Web Studio, Sri Lanka.

PrayerStop helps you find a practical place to pray nearby, using the ACJU prayer timetable, your location and live drive times. This policy explains what the app uses and why. In short: we use your location only to show prayer times and nearby mosques, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Information we use

  • Your location (precise and approximate). Used on your phone to choose the correct ACJU prayer-time zone, and sent to our server to find nearby mosques and drive times. Location is only used while the app is open; it is not tracked in the background.
  • Settings stored on your phone. Your reminder preferences and the last prayer-time zone are saved only on your device so the app works the next time you open it.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or any account information. PrayerStop has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. The app does not record whether you have prayed.

How nearby mosques and drive times are found

When you look for nearby places, the app sends your current coordinates to the PrayerStop server. The server looks up mosques in our own mosque database and, when needed, asks Google Maps Platform (Places and Routes) for nearby mosques and traffic-aware drive times. Google processes those requests under the Google Privacy Policy. The Nearby map is also provided by Google Maps (Android) or Apple Maps (iOS).

We do not store your location on our server or link it to you. Like most online services, our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (which can include the requested location and your IP address) for a short time for security and troubleshooting. Your IP address is also held briefly in memory to limit excessive requests.

Notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders, they are scheduled on your phone from the bundled ACJU timetable. No reminder data is sent to us.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. Your location is only passed to the services described above, solely to provide the app's features.

Security

All communication between the app and our server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • You can turn off location access for PrayerStop at any time in your phone's settings. The app will then ask for location again and cannot show nearby mosques.
  • You can turn off reminders in the app's Settings, or remove all stored settings by uninstalling the app.

Children

PrayerStop is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
